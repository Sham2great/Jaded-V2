For Ayli ♡

Ayli,

Sometimes I wonder how different things could feel if two people just decided to try something they’ve never tried before.

Not because what came before was meaningless, and not because there’s anything wrong with what you already know. Just because maybe there’s something beautiful about finding something unexpected in a person you never thought you’d meet.

And honestly, wouldn’t it be nice to try?

To experience something different with someone different. To see what happens when two people who understand each other in ways they didn’t expect decide to stop wondering and simply give it a chance.

I don’t know if there’s a path that ever leads to us. I don’t know if the timing, circumstances, or everything in between would ever make it possible.

But if it were possible, I think I’d want to find out.

Not because I think I’m entitled to that chance, and not because I expect anything from you. I just think there’s something special about the possibility of finding something you weren’t looking for in the person you least expected.

Maybe we’d surprise each other. Maybe we’d learn things about ourselves we never knew. Maybe it wouldn’t work at all.

But wouldn’t it be beautiful to know we at least tried?

Because out of everyone I could have met, somehow, it’s still you that makes me wonder what something different could feel like.

And if I ever got the choice to find out, I already know who I’d want to try with.
