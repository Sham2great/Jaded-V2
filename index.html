Jade,

I don't really know what to call what I feel for you yet. I just know that I genuinely like you, and that's something I haven't felt in a while.

I know you've been hurt, and I understand why you've built walls around yourself. When you've been disappointed enough times, keeping people at a distance can feel safer than giving someone the chance to hurt you again. I don't want to rush you through that, and I don't want you to feel like you owe me your trust.

I just want the chance to show you who I actually am.

I'm not perfect, but I know my intentions with you are genuine. I'm not here because I want something temporary or because I want to prove I'm different from every guy who's hurt you. I just genuinely enjoy you. There's something about you that makes me want to stay and see where this could go.

And maybe that's why the uncertainty scares me too. I can be patient. I can be honest. I can show you that I'm someone you can trust. But I still don't know if, one day, you'll choose to let me a little closer.

Maybe that's why Drake's “Jaded” hits differently to me — because sometimes the hardest part isn't caring about someone, it's not knowing where you stand with them.

Still, I'm not asking you to force anything. I'm not asking you to suddenly drop every wall you've built. I just want to be somewhere in your life where, eventually, you feel safe enough to let me be myself with you.

Whatever happens, I hope you know that when I say I genuinely like you, I mean it.

You don't have to be perfect for me to think you're worth getting to know.

— Yours, maybe someday.

Whatever happens, I'm glad I met you.
